Statutory Frameworks

Primary legislation enforced in our municipal reviews.

Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Sec. 89)

Places a strict statutory duty on Renfrewshire Council to keep public roads and relevant land clear of litter and refuse.

Roads (Scotland) Act 1984

Mandates local roads authorities to manage, inspect, and maintain public roads and footways in a safe condition.

FOI (Scotland) Act 2002

Used to obtain internal inspection schedules, missed-bin metrics, and departmental maintenance logs.
