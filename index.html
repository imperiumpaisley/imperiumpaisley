Independent Civic Accountability

Evidence over rhetoric.
Accountability over excuses.

Imperium Paisley is an independent, non-partisan civic watchdog focused on public services, local governance and accountability across Paisley and Renfrewshire.

We document issues, examine public records, track responses and advocate for constructive improvements in the public interest.

Our position: Imperium Paisley is currently a civic accountability initiative, not a political party or campaign for electoral office. We scrutinise public decisions and services regardless of party affiliation.

Our Standards

Principles that guide our work

Fair, transparent, proportionate and evidence-based civic scrutiny.

Non-partisan scrutiny

We assess public decisions on their merits, regardless of party affiliation.

Evidence before accusation

An observation is not automatically proof of negligence, misconduct or legal breach.

Constructive accountability

We seek explanations, measurable improvements and transparent public services.

Fairness and right of reply

Relevant authorities should have a reasonable opportunity to respond to disputed claims.

Public interest

Our work concerns public services, public spaces and responsible use of resources.

Corrections and transparency

We aim to correct material errors and distinguish evidence from opinion.

Public Records

FOI & correspondence

Public accountability depends on reliable records, transparent requests and accurate reporting.

Freedom of Information

We may use lawful information requests to seek records concerning public services, decisions and performance.

Request logs should include dates, subjects, authorities and outcomes where appropriate.

Correspondence log

Correspondence should be recorded accurately with reference numbers and relevant dates.

Personal data and confidential information should be redacted before publication.

Response tracking

We distinguish between requests submitted, responses received and actions independently verified.

Response-time statistics should include their source and methodology.

Legal & Evidence Standard

Responsible scrutiny

Imperium Paisley publishes observations and commentary in the public interest. An observation does not, by itself, establish ownership, responsibility, negligence, misconduct or a breach of law.

Legal references are provided for general information and must be checked against the relevant facts and legislation. This website does not provide legal advice.

We aim to distinguish confirmed facts, reported information, opinion and unresolved questions. Material errors may be corrected.

Content must not be used to harass, threaten or target individuals. Public officials may be scrutinised in relation to public duties, but personal attacks and unsupported allegations are not part of this initiative.
